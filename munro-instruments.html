Munro Instruments

Meteorology, air quality and slip resistance, United Kingdom

Founded in 2009

Meteorology, Air Quality & Slip Resistance

Founded in 2009 and part of MRC Group since 2019, Munro Instruments manufactures specialist instruments in Harlow, UK, for meteorology, air quality and slip and skid resistance. Its products include meteorological sensors, air samplers used in the nuclear, chemical, construction and agricultural industries, the Munro Stanley Portable Skid Resistance Tester and anaerobic workstations. It operates a UKAS-accredited laboratory for pendulum calibration and floor slip resistance testing.

Munro Scientific Division

Founded in 2009

Laboratory Equipment

Munro Scientific is the group’s UK laboratory equipment business, based in Harlow alongside Munro Instruments. It develops, manufactures and supplies scientific equipment to customers in more than 70 countries, with a range of over 3,000 laboratory products, including balances, measuring instruments and materials testing equipment, backed by after-sales support.
