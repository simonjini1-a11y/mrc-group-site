Meteo-Tech Ltd

Meteorology and environmental sciences, Israel

Founded in 1989 · Part of MRC Group since 2023

Meteorology & Environmental Sciences

Meteo-Tech Ltd is one of Israel’s leading suppliers of instrumentation and services for meteorology and the environmental sciences. It designs, installs and maintains weather station networks, and supplies equipment for industrial monitoring, air quality, aviation and laboratories. Its environmental consulting services include pollutant dispersion modelling, where it holds patented technologies, as well as forensic meteorology and climate surveys.

Services

  • Design, installation and maintenance of weather station networks
  • Equipment for industrial monitoring, air quality, aviation and laboratories
  • Accredited calibration laboratory (ISO/IEC 17025)
  • Pollutant dispersion modelling, using patented technologies
  • Forensic meteorology and climate surveys
  • Weather observation and forecasting services
