Leadership

The owners and management team leading MRC Group.

Owners

Reuven Bookstein

Co-Chief Executive Officer, Director and Founder

MRC Group

Reuven brings more than 30 years of experience in scientific instrumentation and extensive connections across key industries. He co-founded MRC in 1989 and built it into an international group, acquiring Munro and Meteo-Tech and uniting them with MRC. He leads MRC Group together with Moshe Rachmanov.

Moshe Rachmanov

Co-Chief Executive Officer, Director and Founder

MRC Group

Moshe oversees the group's customer relationships in Israel and internationally, and manages the logistics of importing and exporting products across the group's companies, ensuring reliable supply to customers worldwide.

Senior management

Simon Jini

Chief Technology Officer

MRC Ltd

Simon brings more than ten years of experience in the scientific field, with extensive knowledge of analytical equipment and hands-on experience in industry. He leads automation across the group and holds an M.Sc. in Biochemistry from the Weizmann Institute of Science.
