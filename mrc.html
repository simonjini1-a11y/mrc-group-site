MRC Ltd

Laboratory instruments and equipment, Israel

Founded in 1989

Laboratory Instruments

Founded in 1989, MRC is a leading Israeli supplier of laboratory and industrial equipment. It designs and manufactures laboratory instruments under the MRC brand and represents around 50 leading manufacturers worldwide. Its range covers heating and cooling equipment, environmental chambers, balances, microscopes, centrifuges, measuring instruments and advanced analytical systems, backed by an in-house service center in Holon.
